Grammar & Culture Visual Notes
1. Frequency Adverbs Structure:
Positive (often):
chángcháng + Verb
e.g., chángcháng qù (often go) / chángcháng kàn (often watch)
Negative (seldom / not often):
bù cháng + Verb
e.g., bù cháng lái (seldom come) / bù cháng xuéxí (seldom study)
✘ bù chángcháng (Unnatural! Always use "bù cháng")
2. Demonstratives Comparison: Pronouns vs Place Words (+ "r")
WITHOUT "r" ➔ Pronouns / Modifiers

Refers to things/people. Requires Measure Word:

  • zhè zhāng guāngpán (this CD)
  • nàge lǎoshī (that teacher)
  • nǎge xuésheng? (which student?)
Formula: zhè / nà / nǎ + Measure Word (+ Noun)
WITH "r" ➔ Location / Place Words!

💡 Adding "r" transforms the word into a Place/Location:

  • zhèr (here) ➔ Wǒmen zài zhèr shàngkè. (We have class here.)
  • nàr (there) ➔ Tā qù nàr yóuyǒng. (He goes there to swim.)
  • nǎr (where) ➔ Nǐ xiànzài zài nǎr? (Where are you now?)
3. Culture Note: Liáng Zhù (The Butterfly Lovers / 梁祝)
  • Famous Chinese tragic love story (Liang Shanbo & Zhu Yingtai), often called the Chinese Romeo and Juliet.
  • Tragically separated in life, reunited as butterflies symbolizing eternal love.
  • A "Liáng Zhù" CD refers to the cherished Chinese violin concerto or opera recording.
  • 🎬 Video Reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6MiOTB-Kpzw

Text 1: Dialogue Lines 1–11
1. (Dawei, what are you buying here?)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Dàwéi, nǐ zài zhèr mǎi shénme?

4. (I don't come here often. On Sundays, I often go to the small shopping mall with Lin Na. This mall is very big.)
Mǎ Dàwéi: Wǒ bù cháng lái zhèr. Xīngqītiān wǒ chángcháng gēn Lín Nà qù xiǎo shāngchǎng. Zhège shāngchǎng hěn dà.
5. (What kinds of music do you like?)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Nǐ xǐhuan shénme yīnyuè?
6. (I like Chinese music. How about this CD?)
Mǎ Dàwéi: Wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó yīnyuè. Zhè zhāng guāngpán zěnmeyàng?
7. (This one is very good. It's Butterfly Lovers, very famous.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Zhè zhāng hěn hǎo, shì Liáng Zhù, hěn yǒumíng.
8. (Okay, I'll buy this one. Are there any books or newspapers here?)
Mǎ Dàwéi: Hǎo, wǒ mǎi zhè zhāng. Zhèr yǒu méiyǒu shū hé bàozhǐ?
9. (Here doesn't have books or newspapers.)
Wáng Xiǎoyún: Zhèr méiyǒu shū, yě méiyǒu bàozhǐ.
10. (What about notebooks?)
Mǎ Dàwéi: Běnzi ne?

Grammar & Math Breakdown: Weight & Asking Prices
1. Weight Unit "jīn" Math Conversion:
1 jīn = 500 grams (0.5 kg)

sān jīn píngguǒ
= 3 × 500g
= 1500g (1.5 kg)
liǎng jīn pútao
= 2 × 500g
= 1000g (1.0 kg)
2. Sentence Patterns for Asking & Answering Prices:

Word order is flexible in spoken Chinese, but the core meaning stays identical!

Inquiring / Asking Prices:
  • Yì jīn píngguǒ duōshao qián? (How much is one jin of apples?)
  • Duōshao qián yì jīn (píngguǒ)? (How much per jin for apples?)
  • Píngguǒ yì jīn duōshao qián? (As for apples, how much per jin?)
  • Píngguǒ duōshao qián yì jīn? (How much per jin are apples?)
Answering Prices:
  • Yì jīn píngguǒ sān kuài wǔ. (One jin of apples is 3.50 yuan.)
  • Sān kuài wǔ yì jīn. (3.50 yuan per jin.)
  • Píngguǒ yì jīn sān kuài wǔ. (Apples are 3.50 yuan per jin.)
  • Píngguǒ sān kuài wǔ yì jīn. (Apples are 3.50 yuan per jin.)
  • Sān kuài wǔ. (3.50 yuan.)
3. "duō" Usage Visual:
Adverb (Many / Much)
hěn / zhēn + duō
e.g., hěn duō rén (many people) / zhēn duō (really many)
Question Word (How)
duō + shao / dà
e.g., duōshao qián (how much money) / duō dà (how old)

Spoken Chinese Currency Structure & Omissions (Pg 158)
1. Monetary Unit Structure:
kuài (Yuan)
➔ 10 x
máo (Jiao)
➔ 10 x
fēn (Cent)
Spoken Price Expressions (Formality vs Daily Speech):
• Full Form (Formal):
RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr máo qián (3.20 yuan)
RM 10.55 ➔ shí kuài wǔ máo wǔ fēn qián (10.55 yuan)
• Slightly Complete:
RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr máo
RM 10.55 ➔ shí kuài wǔ máo wǔ fēn
• Most Common Daily Speech (Omission Rule):
RM 3.20 ➔ sān kuài èr
RM 10.55 ➔ shí kuài wǔ máo wǔ
💡 Rule: When "máo" or "fēn" is at the end of an amount phrase, "máo / máo qián" and "fēn / fēn qián" can be omitted.
• Note on "2 máo":
People say èr máo qián or liǎng máo qián (20 cents / 0.20 yuan). When "2 máo" is at the beginning, liǎng máo is often preferred.
2. Adjectives without Adverbs (dà vs hěn dà):

If an adjective DOES NOT have an adverb (like hěn, zhēn) before it, the sentence indicates Comparison!

• Nín kàn, wǒ de píngguǒ dà. (Look, my apples are BIGGER compared to others.)
3. Modern Payment Methods (Extra Knowledge):
💳 shuā kǎ (pay by card) 📱 sǎo mǎ (scan QR code) 💵 xiànjīn (cash)

Text 2: Dialogue Lines 1–18
1. (Sir, what do you want?)
Shīfu: Xiānsheng, nín yào shénme?
2. (Hello, boss. May I ask, what is this?)
Dīng Lìbō: Nín hǎo, shīfu. Qǐngwèn, zhè shì shénme?
3. (You don't recognize it? This is banana apple.)
Shīfu: Nín bú rènshi ma? Zhè shì xiāngjiāo píngguǒ.
4. (Sorry, I'm asking: how do you say this in Chinese?)
Dīng Lìbō: Duìbuqǐ, wǒ shì wèn: Zhège Hànyǔ zěnme shuō?
5. (Ah, you're a foreigner? Where do you work?)
Shīfu: À, nín shì wàiguó rén! Nín zài nǎr gōngzuò?
6. (I study at the Language Institute.)
Dīng Lìbō: Wǒ zài Yǔyán Xuéyuàn xuéxí.
7. (You're studying Chinese, right? Learn with me, it's very easy: this is called banana, this is called banana apple, this is also apple, that is grape...)
Shīfu: Nín xuéxí Hànyǔ, shì bu shì? Nín gēn wǒ xué, hěn róngyì: Zhè jiào xiāngjiāo, zhè jiào xiāngjiāo píngguǒ, zhè yě shì píngguǒ, nà shì pútao...
8. (Banana, apple, banana apple... How much is one jin of apples?)
Dīng Lìbō: Xiāngjiāo, píngguǒ, xiāngjiāo píngguǒ... Yì jīn píngguǒ duōshao qián?
9. (One jin is RM3.20.)
Shīfu: Yì jīn sān kuài èr máo qián.
10. (Your apples are really expensive.)
Dīng Lìbō: Nín de píngguǒ zhēn guì.
11. (RM3.20 per jin is not expensive. Look, my apples are big. Alright, let's be friends, RM3.00 per jin.)
Shīfu: Yì jīn sān kuài èr bú guì. Nín kàn, wǒ de píngguǒ dà. Hǎo, zuò ge péngyou, sān kuài qián yì jīn.
12. (How much is one jin of bananas?)
Dīng Lìbō: Yì jīn xiāngjiāo duōshao qián?
13. (RM2.75 per jin, RM5.00 for two jin.)
Shīfu: Liǎng kuài qī máo wǔ fēn yì jīn, wǔ kuài qián liǎng jīn.
14. (I'll buy three jin of bananas and two jin of apples.)
Dīng Lìbō: Wǒ mǎi sān jīn xiāngjiāo hé liǎng jīn píngguǒ.
15. (The total is RM14.00. I'll also give you one apple for free. Do you want anything else?)
Shīfu: Yígòng shísì kuài qián. Wǒ zài sòng nín yí ge píngguǒ. Nín hái yào shénme?
16. (No more, thank you. Here's the money.)
Dīng Lìbō: Bú yào le, xièxie. Gěi nín qián.
17. (Alright, you gave me RM20.00, I'll give you RM6.00 change. Goodbye.)
Shīfu: Hǎo, nín gěi wǒ èrshí kuài qián, wǒ zhǎo nín liù kuài. Zàijiàn.
18. (Goodbye!)
Dīng Lìbō: Zàijiàn!
